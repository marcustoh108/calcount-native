Consumer health · iOS & Android

YumBalance

Snap your meal. Know your macros.

An AI calorie and macro tracker. Photograph a plate and YumBalance estimates calories, protein, carbs and fat in seconds. As a bonus, it flags any risk the meal may carry for the health conditions and allergies you care about.

  • AI photo and barcode scanning
  • Personalised calorie, macro and weight goals
  • Risk flags for 7 health conditions plus your allergies

From a single photo to a confident decision.

Every product follows the same path: capture the real world, understand it with AI, turn it into structured data, and give people guidance they can act on. Here is that path in YumBalance.

01 · Capture

See the meal

A photo or barcode, taken in the app in seconds.

02 · Understand

Recognise it

A vision model identifies the dish, portion and hidden ingredients like oil and sauces.

03 · Structure

Measure it

The answer becomes validated nutrition data: calories, macros, sodium, sugar and more.

04 · Advise

Act on it

Tracked against your goals, with any risk for your health conditions flagged and explained.
