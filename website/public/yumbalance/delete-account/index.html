YumBalance · Account deletion

Delete your YumBalance account

You can permanently delete your YumBalance account and its data at any time, in the app, on this page, or by email. YumBalance is an app developed by Avencia Private Limited.

Option 1 · In the app

Settings → Delete account & all data

Deletes your account from our servers and erases YumBalance's data from your phone.

Option 2 · On the web

Use the form below

Sign in to confirm it's you, then delete your account. Works even without the app.

Option 3 · By email

Email us

From your account's email address, write to connect@avencia.io with the subject "Delete my YumBalance account". We'll confirm and delete it within 7 days.

If YumBalance is still installed, delete the app to remove the food log and profile stored on your phone. Remember to cancel any subscription in the App Store or Google Play.

Online deletion is briefly unavailable

Please delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account & all data), or email connect@avencia.io from your account's email address with the subject "Delete my YumBalance account". We'll confirm and delete it within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Deleted immediately: your account (email address and securely hashed password) and your account's daily scan counters.
  • Kept for up to 30 days, then deleted: per-phone scan counters, which contain no name or email and are identified only by a random ID. They stop the daily scan limit being reset by deleting and recreating an account.
  • On your phone: your food log, profile, health details and photos are stored only on your phone. Deleting in the app erases them. If you delete on the web, delete the app too to remove them.
  • Not stored by us: meal photos are analysed and not kept on our servers.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, because billing is handled by Apple or Google. Cancel at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions (iPhone) or play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions (Android).
