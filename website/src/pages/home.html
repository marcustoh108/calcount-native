About us

Harnessing AI to solve real-world problems.

We believe the most exciting thing about AI isn't the technology itself, but what people can do with it. So we build products that put it to work.

i.Who

AI-first by design

Avencia is an AI-first technology company. We design, build and run intelligent products end to end, from the models and cloud services behind them to the apps people hold in their hands.

ii.Why

Real problems, solved

We exist to harness the power of AI to solve real-world problems: turning what the latest models can do into simple, dependable tools that make a measurable difference to people's lives and work.

iii.How

Focused, fast, trusted

We build focused products and ship them fast. We put AI exactly where it drives productivity gains and provides powerful insights, with privacy and security engineered in from day one.

Our approach

From frontier models to everyday impact.

Every Avencia product starts with a real problem and a simple question: how can AI make this faster, smarter and easier? Then we engineer the whole experience around the answer.
